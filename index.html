Long-day distance, week by week
Every day
Your baseline plate
  • Carbs: rice, bee hoon, oats, wholemeal bread, sweet potato — your main fuel, don't cut these while training.
  • Protein: chicken, fish, eggs, tofu/tempeh, dhal — aim for a palm-sized portion at each main meal for recovery.
  • Veg + fruit: ulam, stir-fried greens, banana, papaya — banana especially good around training days for potassium.
  • Fluids: 2–3L water daily as a baseline, more on training days. KL humidity means you lose more than you think.
Night before / morning of a long session
Loading up
  • Keep the dinner before simple and carb-forward — plain rice + grilled chicken/fish, or noodle soup. Go easy on oily, heavily spiced, or very fibrous dishes so your gut isn't working hard the next morning.
  • 2–3 hrs before: something familiar and easy to digest — toast with kaya + banana, plain oats, or roti with a bit of honey. Avoid trying new foods before a long effort.
  • Sip water steadily from waking, don't chug right before you start.
Within 45 min after a long session
Recovery window
  • Pair carbs + protein — milo + a sandwich, or nasi + ayam + veg, or a banana + a boiled egg if you're not ready for a full meal yet.
  • Rehydrate with water; if the session ran long or you sweated heavily, coconut water or an isotonic drink helps replace electrolytes better than water alone.
Final 3 days before race
Race week
  • Shift toward more carbs, slightly less fibre — more rice/noodles/bread, lighter on raw veg and very spicy sambal, to keep your stomach calm on race morning.
  • Don't restrict food to "feel light" — your body needs the fuel stored up. This is the one week volume goes up, not down.
  • Stay very consistent with hydration — don't try to "catch up" all at once the day before.
Race morning · Oct 18
Before the gun
  • 2–3 hrs before: a carb meal you've already tested in training — toast + banana + honey, or plain oats. Nothing new, nothing heavy, nothing fried.
  • Small sips of water/isotonic in the last hour, stop chugging ~15 min before start.
  • During the run: sip at water stations rather than skipping — even a few mouthfuls each stop adds up over 15km, especially in KL heat.
This is general guidance, not a strict plan — adjust portions and timing to what actually sits well with you. Let me know if you'd rather I tailor this around halal, vegetarian, or other preferences.
